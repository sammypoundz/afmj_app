Best Email Experiences with SME-scale

SME-scale is an email marketing solution designed for small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) that want to improve their customer engagement and reach. It offers a variety of features such as email automation, segmentation, personalization, and analytics to help SMEs create effective email campaigns that resonate with their audience.

Our Core Features

Send reliably at scale with our online SMTP servers

SMTP is a popular technology used to send emails over the internet, and an SMTP service can be an efficient solution for businesses that need to send large volumes of emails. By using an SMTP service, businesses can save time and resources on server management, and rely on the service provider to ensure reliable email delivery.

Expedite troubleshooting with searchable email activity feed and additional history

By having access to an email activity feed, users can quickly identify any issues that may have occurred and troubleshoot them more efficiently. The additional history feature can provide more context for troubleshooting, allowing users to see a more complete picture of email activity over time.

Increase delivery rates with Email Validation API.

Email Validation API is a service that can verify the validity of email addresses and help prevent undeliverable messages. By using Email Validation API, businesses can reduce their bounce rates and improve their overall email delivery performance.

Collaborate across teams and measure success with an intuitive user interface.

The product is designed to facilitate collaboration across teams, making it easier to work together on projects and achieve shared goals. The intuitive user interface can help users navigate the product more easily, reducing the learning curve and increasing overall productivity.

24/7 Email tracking system

The email tracking system is designed to monitor email activity around the clock, providing real-time updates on when emails are sent, received, and opened. By using this tool, businesses can gain valuable insights into their email performance, including open rates, click-through rates, and other key metrics.

Create emails with HTML code, a drag-and-drop editor, or a combination of both.

By offering multiple options for email creation, the product can cater to a wider range of users with varying levels of technical expertise. Users can choose to work with HTML code for greater flexibility and control, or use the drag-and-drop editor for a more user-friendly experience.
